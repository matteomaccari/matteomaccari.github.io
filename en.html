The method

A structured conversation that starts with you.

I use the GROW model: a simple, flexible framework that takes you from goal to action. I don't tell you what to do. With the right questions, I help you find your own answer.

G

Goal

We clarify what you want to achieve, from the session and from the journey.

What would you like to be different?
R

Reality

We explore where things stand today: facts, resources, obstacles, what you've already tried.

What is happening right now?
O

Options

We bring the alternatives to the surface before choosing one.

What else could you do?
W

Will

You define the next step, with a concrete timeline and commitment.

What will you do, and when?

The structure is a guide, not a questionnaire: if something new comes up during the session, we can step back and reframe the goal.

About me

Frequently asked questions

Before you start

What is coaching, and what isn't it?

Coaching is focused on the present and the future: it starts from a goal and helps you reach it using your own resources. It is neither therapy nor consulting: I don't diagnose and I don't tell you what to do. If needs of a different kind come up, I will tell you openly.

What happens in the first session?

We get to know each other, you clarify what brings you to coaching and we define the goal together. From the very first session you leave with a clearer picture and at least one concrete next step.

60 or 80 minutes?

The price is the same, so choose what works for you: 60 minutes for a focused session, 80 if you prefer more room to explore. You can change the length each time.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goal. Some people find what they are looking for in a few sessions, others prefer a longer journey. There is no minimum: you decide after each meeting.

How do I book and receive the session link?

Pick a date and time from the calendar in the Book section. After booking you will receive a confirmation email with all the details, and you can add the appointment to your own calendar.

Are sessions held online?

Yes, sessions take place online by video call, so you can join from wherever suits you best.
